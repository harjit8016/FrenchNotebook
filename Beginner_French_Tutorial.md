# Essential Beginner French Hacks & Quick Reference

A structured, zero-fluff reference guide tailored for learners with an **English & Punjabi** background. It focuses on the most essential hacks: Special Characters, Gender Rules, 500+ Cognate Conversions, Silent Letters, Core Verbs, and Everyday Survival Sentences.

---

## 1. Special Characters & Accents Hack

French accents change pronunciation or distinguish word meanings:

| Special Character | Name | English Sound Equivalent | Punjabi Sound Hint | Example Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **é** | Accent Aigu | "ay" in *day* | ਏ | une **é**cole | a school |
| **è / ê** | Accent Grave / Circonflexe | "eh" in *bed* | ਐ / ਏ | la m**è**re, la f**ê**te | mother, party |
| **ç** | C-cedilla | Soft "S" before a, o, u | ਸ | gar**ç**on | boy |
| **à / où** | Accent Grave (a, u) | Keeps sound, changes meaning | ਆ / ਊ | **à** (at/to), **où** (where) | contrast with *a* (has), *ou* (or) |

## 2. Gender Hacks (Masculine vs Feminine Nouns)

Instead of memorizing gender for every word, use ending patterns:

- **Feminine Endings (Use *la* / *une*)**:
  - Words ending in **-tion**, **-té**, **-ette**, **-ence** are almost ALWAYS feminine.
  - *Examples*: *la nation*, *la santé* (health), *la serviette* (towel).
- **Masculine Endings (Use *le* / *un*)**:
  - Words ending in **-ment**, **-eau**, **-age**, **-isme** are almost ALWAYS masculine.
  - *Examples*: *le gouvernement*, *le bateau* (boat), *le fromage* (cheese).
- **The 'E' Rule Trick**: Most nouns ending in **-e** tend to be feminine, except major daily words like *le livre* (book) and *le musée* (museum).

## 3. 500+ Word Cognate Hacks (Instant Vocabulary)

Convert English words to French using these ending rules:

| English Rule | French Ending | English Word | French Word | Punjabi Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **-tion → -tion** | Same spelling! | nation / attention | **nation** / **attention** | ਨਾਸਿਓਂ / ਆਤਾਂਸਿਓਂ |
| **-or → -eur** | Change -or to -eur | doctor / actor | **docteur** / **acteur** | ਦੌਕਤਰ / ਆਕਤਰ |
| **-ty → -té** | Change -ty to -té | university / liberty | **université** / **liberté** | ਊਨੀਵੇਰਸਿਤੇ / ਲਿਬੇਰਤੇ |
| **-ic → -ique** | Change -ic to -ique | music / logic | **musique** / **logique** | ਮੂਜ਼ੀਕ / ਲੋਜ਼ੀਕ |
| **-ous → -eux** | Change -ous to -eux | famous / dangerous | **fameux** / **dangereux** | ਫਾਮੂ / ਦਾਂਜਰੂ |
| **-able → -able** | Same spelling! | comfortable / adorable | **confortable** / **adorable** | ਕੋਂਫੋਰਤਾਬਲ / ਆਦੋਰਾਬਲ |

## 4. Silent Letters & Pronunciation Rules

- **The DEPSTX Rule**: Final consonants **D, E, P, S, T, X** are SILENT at the end of French words.
  - *petit* → pronounced *puh-tee* (silent t)
  - *grand* → pronounced *grahn* (silent d)
- **Liaison Rule**: When a silent final consonant meets a word starting with a vowel next, the silent consonant wakes up as a smooth transition sound:
  - *les amis* → pronounced *leh-zah-mee* (silent s becomes a Z sound).
- **eau / au Sound**: Always pronounced like a clean English "O" (*eau* = water, *bateau* = boat).

## 6. Essential Conjugations (The Big 4 Verbs)

| Pronoun | **Être** (to be) | **Avoir** (to have) | **Aller** (to go) | **Parler** (-ER pattern) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **je (j')** | suis *(I am)* | ai *(I have)* | vais *(I go)* | parle *(I speak)* |
| **tu** | es *(you are)* | as *(you have)* | vas *(you go)* | parles *(you speak)* |
| **il/elle/on** | est *(he/she is)* | a *(he/she has)* | va *(he/she goes)* | parle *(he/she speaks)* |
| **nous** | sommes *(we are)* | avons *(we have)* | allons *(we go)* | parlons *(we speak)* |
| **vous** | êtes *(you are)* | avez *(you have)* | allez *(you go)* | parlez *(you speak)* |
| **ils/elles** | sont *(they are)* | ont *(they have)* | vont *(they go)* | parlent *(they speak)* |

> 💡 **Age Hack:** In French, express age with **Avoir** (e.g., *J'ai 29 ans* = literally "I have 29 years").

## 7. Everyday Survival Sentences (With English & Punjabi Phonetics)

| French Sentence | English Meaning | Phonetic Sound |
|---|---|---|
| **Bonjour ! Je m'appelle Harjit.** | Hello! My name is Harjit. | *Bon-zhoor ! Zhuh mah-pell Harjit · ਬੋਂਜੂਰ ! ਯ਼ ਮਾਪੈਲ ਹਰਜੀਤ* |
| **Je suis ingénieur logiciel.** | I am a software engineer. | *Zhuh swee zahn-zhay-nyur loh-zhee-syell · ਯ਼ੂ ਸ੍ਵੀ ਇੰਜੀਨੀਅਰ* |
| **Une table pour deux personnes, s'il vous plaît.** | A table for two people, please. | *Oon tab-luh poor duh pair-sonn, seel voo pleh · ਊਨ ਤਾਬਲ* |
| **Je voudrais un café au lait.** | I would like a coffee with milk. | *Zhuh voo-dreh un kah-fay oh leh · ਯ਼ ਵੂਦਰੇ ਐਨ ਕਾਫੇ* |
| **L'addition, s'il vous plaît.** | The bill, please. | *Lah-dee-syohn, seel voo pleh · ਲਾਦੀਸਿਓਂ* |
| **Excusez-moi, où se trouve la gare ?** | Excuse me, where is the train station? | *Ex-koo-zay mwah, oo suh troov lah gahr ? · ਐਕਸਕੂਜ਼ੇ ਮੁਆ* |
| **Aidez-moi, s'il vous plaît !** | Help me, please! | *Ay-day mwah, seel voo pleh ! · ਐਦੇ ਮੁਆ* |
